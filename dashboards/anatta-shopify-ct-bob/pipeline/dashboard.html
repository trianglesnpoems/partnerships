Step one

What a good fit means, for each side

The two ICPs share very little vocabulary. An account can be obvious to one of us and invisible to the other.

Shopify’s ICP

Read off Shopify’s 2026 enterprise positioning and what its account executives are paid to land.

What they sell
Shopify Plus and Shopify Enterprise: one commerce foundation for B2C, B2B and retail. Checkout and Shop Pay, POS, B2B catalogues and terms, Markets for international, Hydrogen for headless, Flow for automation.
Who they want
Brands and retailers with real online revenue (roughly $1M+ online for Plus, $50M+ total for Enterprise) that are on a legacy platform, a mid-market platform they have outgrown, or no proper storefront at all.
Where it lands cleanly
Replatforms off Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Adobe Commerce / Magento, Oracle, SAP, VTEX or a custom build; unifying stores and online on POS; adding a B2B catalogue; consolidating several storefronts.
What winning looks like
A signed Plus or Enterprise merchant whose launch an SI like Anatta can deliver in a quarter, with POS, B2B or Markets attached.
What kills a deal
Government or military procurement, a business with no product catalogue, a brand already on Plus (that is expansion, not a win), or a category Shopify cannot host.

Anatta’s ICP

Our own book, expressed the same way so the two are comparable.

What we build
High-performance Shopify Plus and headless (Hydrogen) storefronts for DTC and omnichannel brands: migrations, builds, then conversion, subscription and LTV optimisation on a retainer. Shopify Platinum Partner.
Who buys
Brands with a product catalogue, a real ecommerce team and enough scale to sustain an embedded team. Multi-location, multi-brand or subscription complexity is a plus.
The signal we already run
FIRE, an account-level fit and intent score combining Shopify, replatform and agency-search intent, engagement recency and in-market status. It enters this model as a 20-point intent subscore computed straight from the export.
What winning looks like
A replatform or modernisation engagement at a brand whose current stack is holding back conversion or repeat revenue, ideally alongside Shopify’s own deal team.
Proof
Rothy’s from $0 to $220M, Molekule through to IPO, AG1, Dollar Shave Club, Mack Weldon.
