How to read it

Two scores, one blend, hard gates

Each account gets a Shopify-fit score from a six-part rubric built from what Shopify sells to enterprise brands in 2026 (platform whitespace, scale, business model, unified-commerce surface, timing, ecosystem pull), and an Anatta-fit score from a four-part rubric plus the FIRE intent signal we already maintain. The two blend 55/45 toward Shopify because the partner's fit is the harder-won signal. Anything government, distressed, or without a commerce surface is gated out before tiering, with a reason in plain English; anything already on Shopify Plus moves to an expansion tier instead of being scored as a prospect.

    Step one

    What "a good fit" means, for each side

    The two ICPs share very little vocabulary, which is the point: an account can be obvious to one of us and invisible to the other.

    Shopify's ICP

    Read off Shopify's 2026 enterprise positioning and what its account executives are paid to land.

    What they sell
    Shopify Plus and Shopify Enterprise: one commerce foundation for B2C, B2B and retail — checkout and Shop Pay, POS, B2B catalogues and terms, Markets for international, Hydrogen for headless, Flow for automation.
    Who they want
    Brands and retailers with real online revenue (roughly $1M+ online for Plus, $50M+ total for Enterprise) that are on a legacy platform, a mid-market platform they've outgrown, or no proper storefront at all.
    Where it lands cleanly
    Replatforms off Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Adobe Commerce / Magento, Oracle, SAP, VTEX or a custom build; unifying stores + online on POS; adding a B2B catalogue; consolidating multiple storefronts.
    What winning looks like
    A signed Plus/Enterprise merchant whose launch an SI like Anatta can actually deliver in a quarter, with POS/B2B/Markets attached.
    What kills a deal
    Government or military procurement, a business with no product catalogue, a brand already on Plus (that's expansion, not a win), or a category Shopify can't host.

    Anatta's ICP

    Our own book, expressed the same way so the two are comparable.

    What we build
    High-performance Shopify Plus and headless (Hydrogen) storefronts for DTC and omnichannel brands — migrations, builds, then conversion, subscription and LTV optimisation on a retainer. Shopify Platinum Partner.
    Who buys
    Brands with a product catalogue, a real ecommerce team and enough scale to sustain an embedded team — multi-location, multi-brand or subscription complexity is a plus, not a problem.
    The signal we already run
    FIRE — an account-level fit and intent score combining Shopify / replatform / agency-search intent, engagement recency and in-market status. It enters this model as a 20-point intent subscore computed straight from the export.
    What winning looks like
    A replatform or modernisation engagement at a brand whose current stack is holding back conversion or repeat revenue — ideally alongside Shopify's own deal team.
    Proof
    Rothy's from $0 to $220M, Molekule through to IPO, AG1, Dollar Shave Club, Mack Weldon.
