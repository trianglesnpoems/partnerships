Senior Partnerships Manager

About the role
Fabrikam is hiring a Senior Partnerships Manager to grow our agency partner program. You will own agency partner relationships across the Shopify ecosystem, run co-selling motions with our sales team, and report on partner-sourced pipeline.

What you will do
- Recruit, onboard and enable agency partners in the Shopify ecosystem
- Run account mapping with agency partners and turn overlaps into co-selling plays
- Own partner-sourced pipeline targets and report on them in Salesforce
- Plan joint events and co-marketing with agency partners
- Work with product on integrations that agency partners ask for

What we are looking for
- 5+ years in partnerships or channel sales at a SaaS company
- Direct experience with Shopify agency partners
- A track record of partner-sourced pipeline, with numbers
- Comfortable with Salesforce and account mapping tools such as Crossbeam
- Clear writing and stakeholder management across sales and product

Location: Remote (US)
Compensation: $140,000 - $170,000 base plus variable
